Debt review removal

Removal is not a generic cancellation service.

The right procedure depends on what happened in your debt review: whether you applied, whether a determination or order exists, and whether the qualifying debts have been settled.

Understand your options →
Route A

Clearance certificate

For eligible consumers who have completed the relevant repayment obligations and meet the statutory clearance requirements.

Route B

Court-based relief

Potentially relevant where a binding order or other legal status must be dealt with by a court, rather than simply updated administratively.

Route C

Incorrect / irregular listing

For files where the consumer disputes consent, process compliance or the legal foundation for the debt review listing.

Common questions

What clients usually ask first.

No service provider can lawfully promise a one-click cancellation. The correct route depends on the stage of the debt review, whether Form 17.2 was issued, whether a court or Tribunal order exists, and whether the relevant debts have been settled.

A section 71 clearance certificate is generally the cleanest route after the qualifying obligations under the debt rearrangement have been discharged. The certificate is issued by the appropriate NCR-registered debt counsellor and is then used to update the credit record.
