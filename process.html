Process & procedure

A guided path from “I’m stuck” to a clear legal next step.

Our process is built for speed through preparation: identify the correct route early, collect the right evidence once and keep every hand-off visible.

The client journey

Your sales consultant becomes your case guide.

Your consultant coordinates intake, explains what happens next and keeps you informed while the appropriate professional handles regulated legal or debt-counselling steps.

01

Listen & qualify

We confirm where you are in the debt review process, what has been paid and whether a court or Tribunal order exists.

02

Build your file

You receive a short case-specific checklist for paid-up letters, credit reports, statements and legal documents.

03

Select the lawful route

The file is triaged for clearance processing, correction of an improper listing or court-based relief where appropriate.

04

Coordinate the process

We keep the hand-offs moving between you and the relevant debt counsellor, legal professionals, credit providers or bureaus.

05

Follow through

We track the matter to the lawful completion point and show you what to verify on your updated credit profile.

Procedure map

What happens behind the scenes.

Each matter branches according to its legal status so clients do not pay for the wrong procedure.

1

Status verification

Credit profile, debt review history, settlement position and order status are checked.

2

Route confirmation

We classify the matter for clearance, correction or court-based assessment.

3

Document completion

Missing paid-up letters, account statements and legal records are identified.

4

Formal action

The appropriate regulated party takes the required statutory, administrative or legal step.

5

Bureau update & verification

Once a lawful basis exists, credit-profile updates are followed up and checked.
