The principle

First determine your status. Then act.

Debt review under the National Credit Act is a formal process. The correct exit route depends on how far the process went and what documents or orders already exist. Libra Legal SA starts with a status assessment rather than a blanket “cancellation” promise.

Important: A debt review flag is not removed merely because payments stop or a fee is paid. The lawful route may require clearance, correction or court action depending on the facts.

We assess

  • Whether a Form 16 application was made
  • Whether an over-indebted determination was issued
  • Whether a court or Tribunal order exists
  • Which accounts are paid up
  • What the credit report currently shows
